INTERNAL CASE ROUTING POLICY (v1)

Risk-level bands and required routing:

- LOW (risk score 0.00-0.34): Auto-close / monitor. No further action
  needed immediately. Log the decision and retain evidence for 90 days
  in case of future pattern matches.
- MEDIUM (risk score 0.35-0.64): Route to human review queue. A licensed
  fraud investigator must review within 5 business days.
- HIGH (risk score 0.65-1.00): Escalate immediately. Freeze any pending
  payout, notify the compliance officer, and prepare a Suspicious
  Activity Report for regulatory filing within the statutory window.

OVERRIDE RULE: any case where the fraud-ring subgraph contains 3 or more
connected claimants sharing a strong entity (account or device) is
escalated to HIGH regardless of the computed risk score. Note: shared
address alone does NOT trigger this override -- shared housing (e.g.
family members, a shared mailing address) is common among legitimate
claimants and is treated as weaker, contributory evidence rather than a
hard trigger (see KB-002's caveat on shared infrastructure).

OVERRIDE RULE: any case with a document validation finding of a
duplicate invoice number shared across 2 or more claims is routed to at
least MEDIUM regardless of the computed risk score, and to HIGH if the
duplicate spans 3 or more claims.

OVERRIDE RULE: any case with a confirmed claim-amount-vs-invoice-amount
mismatch is routed to at least MEDIUM (human review) regardless of the
computed risk score, since this is a specific, well-defined finding that
should not be diluted by other quiet signals.

OVERRIDE RULE: any case with a confirmed high-velocity transaction burst
(3+ transactions by the same entity within the trailing window) is
routed to at least MEDIUM (human review) regardless of the computed
risk score, for the same reason.

These thresholds are configurable operational policy, not statutory
requirements, and should be reviewed periodically against measured
false-positive and false-negative rates.
